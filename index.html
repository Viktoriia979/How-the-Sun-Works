How the Sun Works:

The Sun is a huge ball of extremely hot gas, mainly made up of hydrogen and helium. It produces energy through a process called nuclear fusion, which happens in its core. During fusion, hydrogen atoms join together to form helium and release a huge amount of energy as heat and light. This energy travels from the Sun through space and reaches Earth as sunlight. The Sun’s energy is important because it provides the heat and light needed for life on Earth and helps plants carry out photosynthesis. The Sun has been producing energy for about 4.6 billion years and is expected to continue doing so for billions more years.
